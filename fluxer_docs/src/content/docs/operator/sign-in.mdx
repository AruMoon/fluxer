title: Sign-in methods
description: Usernames or email addresses, single sign-on, recovery kits and reset links on a self-hosted instance.
---

People sign in to your instance with a username or with an email address. You choose one during setup, and it can't change once the first account exists.

## Usernames

People create an account with a username and a password. The instance never asks for an email address and doesn't need to send email. Features that send email are off, such as email verification and password resets by email.

When someone forgets their password, they use their recovery kit. The app shows the kit right after the account is created, ready to download or print, and people can make a new one in their account settings. Using the kit sets a new password, signs the account out everywhere and gives the person a new kit to keep.

## Email addresses

People create an account with an email address, a username and a password. The instance sends email to verify addresses and to reset forgotten passwords, and rexctl asks for your email server during setup. To change it later, run `rexctl email set`.

Until email is set up, the instance can't check addresses, and nobody can reset a forgotten password by email.

## Single sign-on

People can sign in through your own identity provider, such as Authentik or Keycloak, over OpenID Connect. Set it up in the admin panel on the "Instance Config" page, in the "Single Sign-On (SSO)" card. The card shows the redirect URI to give your provider.

With "Automatically provision users on first SSO login" on, the first sign-in from someone new creates their account. In single-community mode, "Community Roles From Groups" gives people roles in the community from their groups at your provider.

"Require SSO" turns off passwords, passkeys and registration for everyone. Before you turn it on, make sure at least one admin can sign in through your provider.

## Lost passwords

When someone has lost their password and can't reset it themselves, create a reset link for them:

```sh
rexctl users reset-link alex
```

Anyone with the link can set a new password for the account until the link expires. Send it to the person over a channel you trust. The admin panel has the same action as "Create Password Reset Link" on the account's page.

Keep the recovery kit for your own admin account somewhere safe, and add a passkey or a second admin account. If you still lose access, `rexctl users reset-link` works on the machine without signing in.

## More settings

The "Sign-in & new accounts" card on the "Instance Config" page has the other settings for new accounts, such as instant accounts and when unused accounts are deleted.
